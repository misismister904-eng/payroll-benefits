# Design brief — Civic Ledger

## Direction candidates

1. **Civic Ledger** — calm navy/teal, clear administrative surfaces, practical tables and forms. Probability 0.08.
2. **People Operations** — warmer blue/indigo with approachable cards and human-centered employee cues. Probability 0.07.
3. **Payroll Studio** — charcoal/royal-blue with compact information density and strong computation emphasis. Probability 0.06.

## Committed direction

Civic Ledger is the chosen direction for the Archon Nell capstone UI.

- **Movement:** calm institutional utility with light editorial hierarchy.
- **Core principles:** clarity over decoration, visible state, approachable forms, honest prototype boundaries.
- **Color philosophy:** deep navy for trust, teal for action and attendance flow, green/amber/red only for statuses, warm off-white canvas.
- **Layout paradigm:** persistent desktop sidebar, compact top bar, generous content column, cards for summaries and scroll-safe tables for records.
- **Signature elements:** small teal accent rail, computation cards with step labels, clear RUNNING/FINAL status chips, attendance-to-payroll flow ribbon.
- **Interaction philosophy:** direct, low-motion, immediate local feedback; every action communicates loading, success, error, empty or confirmation state.
- **Animation:** short opacity/translate transitions only for feedback; no decorative motion.
- **Typography system:** system sans stack, strong numeric tabular figures for payroll values, compact uppercase labels.
- **Brand essence:** dependable, organized, people-aware payroll administration for Archon Nell Incorporated.
- **Brand voice:** clear, professional, reassuring, student-capstone realistic.
- **Wordmark/logo:** Archon Nell Incorporated wordmark paired with a flat payroll ledger mark and a teal check accent.
- **Signature brand color:** Archon Navy `#0f2747`, with Payroll Teal `#0e8f83`.

## Prototype boundary

This is a frontend-only prototype. Sample data is illustrative and lives in `src/data/mockData.ts`. Authentication, database persistence, official deduction rates, server-side RBAC, and payroll APIs are future integration points described by typed service boundaries only.
